01 / PURPOSE

A statistical lens on employee turnover.

Learn from historical HR observations, estimate a binary outcome and communicate the result without treating a prediction as a definitive explanation.

Pattern discovery

Examine associations between tenure, satisfaction, overtime, compensation and the recorded attrition outcome. Logistic Regression models linear relationships in the transformed feature space.

Probability estimation

Logistic Regression can return a probability estimate for the attrition class. Its reliability depends on evaluation, calibration and how closely future data resemble training data.

Decision support

Use analytical findings to inform research and workforce planning. Predictions should never replace employee conversations, context or responsible human judgment.

02 / MODEL INPUTS

Inside the feature space.

The original IBM HR dataset includes employee, role, tenure, satisfaction and compensation attributes. The precise input schema depends on the features retained by the trained pipeline.

01 / CONTEXT

Personal & work context

Location, travel and work arrangements

  • Age — Recorded age in years
  • BusinessTravel — Travel frequency category
  • DistanceFromHome — Recorded home-to-work distance
  • Gender — Recorded gender category
  • MaritalStatus — Recorded marital status
  • OverTime — Whether overtime is recorded
02 / ORGANIZATION

Role & structure

Professional and organizational attributes

  • Department — Business unit
  • EducationField — Field of education
  • JobRole — Role classification
  • JobLevel — Recorded organizational level
  • Education — Education category
  • NumCompaniesWorked — Previous companies count
03 / EXPERIENCE

Career & tenure

Professional history and progression

  • TotalWorkingYears — Total working experience
  • YearsAtCompany — Tenure at the organization
  • YearsInCurrentRole — Time in the current role
  • YearsSinceLastPromotion — Time since promotion
  • YearsWithCurrManager — Time with current manager
04 / ENGAGEMENT

Satisfaction & engagement

Recorded survey and workplace indicators

  • EnvironmentSatisfaction — Workplace environment rating
  • JobInvolvement — Job involvement rating
  • JobSatisfaction — Job satisfaction rating
  • RelationshipSatisfaction — Relationship rating
  • WorkLifeBalance — Work-life balance rating
  • StockOptionLevel — Stock option category
05 / REWARD

Compensation & development

Pay, training and performance indicators

  • MonthlyIncome — Recorded monthly income
  • PercentSalaryHike — Salary increase percentage
  • PerformanceRating — Performance rating category
  • TrainingTimesLastYear — Training sessions last year
06 / DATA QUALITY

Preparation & exclusions

Feature selection and preprocessing

  • Attrition — Binary target label
  • EmployeeNumber — Record identifier, not a meaningful predictor
  • Constant fields — EmployeeCount, Over18 and StandardHours
  • Rate variables — Review DailyRate, HourlyRate and MonthlyRate using validation, not appearance alone
  • Preprocessing — Encoding and scaling fitted on training data

Schema note: the dataset contains 35 original columns, including the target and EmployeeNumber. After removing EmployeeNumber, Attrition, EmployeeCount, Over18 and StandardHours, 30 candidate predictor columns remain. The exact final feature count depends on the actual feature-selection decisions. Removing a rate variable requires a defensible validation result; a low-looking plot alone does not establish that it should be dropped.

03 / PIPELINE

From employee data to model output.

A repeatable inference workflow keeps feature transformations consistent between model training and prediction.

01

Data preparation

Validate required fields, expected data types and allowed categories before constructing the input record.

02

Feature transformation

Apply the saved preprocessing pipeline, including categorical encoding and numerical scaling fitted on training data.

03

Model inference

Pass the transformed features to the trained Logistic Regression estimator to obtain a predicted class and, where supported, a class probability.

04

Result interpretation

Present the returned result with suitable context, measured performance and a clear explanation of model limitations.

04 / GOVERNANCE

Responsible use is part of the model.

Model performance, fairness, privacy and appropriate human oversight are necessary considerations when analyzing employee data.

Statistical estimator notice

AttritionIQ is an educational machine learning application based on historical patterns in the IBM HR Analytics benchmark dataset. Historical associations do not establish causation, and results may not generalize to other organizations, time periods or workforce populations.

Responsible-use constraint: This prototype is not validated for operational personnel decisions. Do not use its predictions alone to determine hiring, compensation, promotion, performance reviews or termination. Before any real-world use, conduct rigorous performance and fairness evaluations, establish data privacy safeguards, assess limitations and maintain meaningful human oversight.
